SalluNet

A 15 MB background-removal model for people. 4.03M parameters, distilled from BiRefNet. It runs entirely in this browser tab — your photo is never uploaded anywhere.

On 500 held-out portraits it produces 45% more accurate hair edges than rembg (a 168 MB model) and 18% more accurate than RVM, the closest competitor by size. It only does humans — that narrowness is exactly why it can be this small.

Honest limitations

  • People only. Give it a car or a dog and the output is meaningless.
  • Still images. No temporal consistency, so it will flicker on video.
  • Benchmarked in-domain. The eval set and most training data come from the same portrait dataset, so real-world photos may be harder than the numbers suggest.
  • Hard cases stay hard. Fine flyaway hair against a busy background, heavy backlighting, and motion blur all degrade the matte.

Against rembg and RVM, on the same photo

All three models run identically — squashed to 512², alpha resized back. Top row is the cutout over a checkerboard, bottom row the same hair crop enlarged. rembg and RVM both keep a slab of sky as a halo.

This is a quality comparison only — RVM is 2.1× faster than SalluNet. Three more sheets are in the repo README.
